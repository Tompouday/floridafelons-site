Florida Felons is a small publishing team. We find the best moments from Twitch, edit them into short-form clips and longer compilations, and publish them to our own channels.

Are you in one of our clips and want it gone?

Email gta.content.six@gmail.com and it comes down within one hour. No reason needed, no argument, and we add you to a permanent list so we never feature you again.

How we work

  • Everyone gets credited. On screen for the whole clip, in the description, and in the post text.
  • We clip small and mid-sized streamers on purpose. The exposure is worth something to them, which is the entire point.
  • Removals are honoured within the hour, every time, no questions.
  • No leaked or pre-release footage, ever, however it was obtained.

Want to be featured?

Send us your own clips and we will put them in front of a new audience, with credit and a link back to your channel. Email gta.content.six@gmail.com.
