Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Who we are

Florida Felons is a small publishing team that produces edited gaming highlight videos for our own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram channels. Contact: gta.content.six@gmail.com

What we collect

From clip contributors who use our submission form:

  • The clip URL you submit
  • Your streaming channel handle (Twitch or YouTube)
  • The display name you want credited
  • Your email address
  • Any description you choose to add
  • The date and time of your submission, your IP address, and a record of which version of our terms you agreed to

From visitors to this website: nothing. These pages are static, we run no analytics and we set no cookies. Our submission form, when it is live, is hosted separately and keeps standard server request logs (IP address, user agent, page requested) for 30 days for security and troubleshooting.

We do not collect payment details, government identifiers, precise location data, or any special category personal data. We do not use advertising trackers.

How we verify clip ownership

When you submit a Twitch clip, we query the public Twitch API to check whether the clip's broadcaster matches the channel handle you gave us. We do this so that we only publish clips with permission from the person who actually recorded them. This check uses only publicly available Twitch data and does not require access to your Twitch account.

Who we share it with

  • Platform APIs. When we publish a video, the caption and description, including your credit and handle, are sent to YouTube, TikTok or Instagram as part of that post.
  • Service providers. Our hosting provider (Amazon Web Services, Ireland), this site's host (GitHub Pages), and the AI provider we use to help order and caption videos (Anthropic). Clip descriptions may be processed by these services. Your email address is not.
  • Nobody else. We do not sell personal data and we do not share it for advertising.

Where your data is held

On servers in Ireland (EU). Where a service provider processes data outside the EEA, that transfer is covered by Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Submission records including the licence you granted: kept for as long as the video remains published, plus six years. We need this to prove we had permission.
  • Email addresses: deleted on request, unless we still need the submission record above.
  • Server logs: 30 days.
  • Exclusion list entries: kept permanently. If you ask us never to feature you again, we have to keep that record in order to honour it.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict how we use it, or object to our use of it. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Email gta.content.six@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Removing your clip. You do not need to make a formal request. Email gta.content.six@gmail.com and we will take the video down as fast as we can, usually the same day, with no reason required and no argument.

We will keep a record of your handle on our permanent exclusion list, which is how we make sure we never feature you again.

Children

Our submission form is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe we have, email gta.content.six@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. Material changes affecting clip contributors will be emailed to the address on file.
