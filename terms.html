1. What this covers

Florida Felons produces edited gaming highlight videos and publishes them to its own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram channels. These terms cover anyone who submits a clip to us. If you do not submit clips, only the Privacy Policy applies to you.

2. Submitting a clip

When you submit a clip you confirm that:

  • The clip is your own recording, from your own stream or capture.
  • You are 18 or over.
  • You have the right to let us use it.

We check this automatically where we can. For Twitch clips we query the public Twitch API to confirm the clip's broadcaster matches the channel handle you gave us. Submissions that do not match are rejected.

Submitting someone else's clip is not allowed, even if you made the clip on their stream. Permission has to come from the person who was broadcasting.

3. The licence you grant us

By submitting a clip you give Florida Felons permission to edit it and publish it on our YouTube, TikTok and Instagram channels, with credit to you.

You keep ownership of your clip. This licence is non-exclusive: you can keep using your own footage anywhere else, including on your own channels, with no restriction from us. You are not being paid for this, and we are not promising that your clip will be used.

What we may do to your clip

  • Trim it, reframe it to vertical, and add on-screen text
  • Include it in a compilation alongside other creators' clips
  • Publish it on any of our channels

What we will not do

  • Claim ownership of your clip
  • Sell your clip or license it onward to anyone else
  • Use it in a way that presents you as endorsing a product
  • Stop you using your own footage however you like

4. Credit

Every clip we publish is credited to the original broadcaster on screen for the duration of the clip, in the video description, and in the post text where the platform allows it.

5. Removal

Email gta.content.six@gmail.com and we will take the video down within one hour. No reason needed. No argument.

This applies whether you submitted the clip yourself or we found it. We will also add you to a permanent exclusion list so that we never feature you again. That list is the one record we keep after a removal, and we keep it precisely so we can honour your request in future.

6. Clips we source ourselves

Some clips are selected editorially from publicly shared Twitch clips rather than submitted to us. Where we do this we credit the original creator on screen, in the description and in the post, and we honour any removal request under section 5 on the same one-hour basis.

We do not publish footage from leaked or pre-release builds of any game, however it was obtained.

7. Acceptable use

Do not submit clips that contain illegal content, content involving minors, personal information about other people, or material you do not have the right to share. We reject submissions at our discretion and we do not have to explain why.

8. No warranty

This site and our channels are provided as they are. We do not guarantee that your clip will be featured, that any video stays published, or that the site is available at any particular time.

9. Changes

We may update these terms. The version string at the top changes when we do, and we record which version each submission agreed to, so a change never applies retroactively to a clip you already sent us.
